The Challenge

Aura Jewelry, an elegant jewelry brand based in the United Arab Emirates, wanted to launch an online storefront. Without an existing website, they had no digital infrastructure, no online payment gateways, and no branding elements tailored for the local luxury eCommerce market.

They needed a high-performance, conversion-optimized storefront built from scratch, alongside a launch campaign strategy to acquire their first digital sales in a highly competitive market.

The Strategy

Storify designed and executed a complete end-to-end storefront build and paid marketing campaign:

  • Custom Shopify Storefront: Built a premium, lightning-fast Shopify store with a sleek minimalist theme, customized product display pages, and local currency support.
  • Visual Brand Identity: Structured their brand color scheme, typography, high-end packaging designs, and digital assets.
  • TikTok Spark Campaigns: Produced and scaled premium lifestyle aesthetic videos for TikTok Spark ads targeting UAE jewelry buyers.

The Execution

We developed the storefront with a heavy focus on mobile responsiveness and conversion-friendly features. We integrated local UAE payment providers and optimized checkout steps to enable quick 1-click purchases.

On the marketing side, we partnered with local creators to generate realistic jewelry unboxing and styling reels. These videos were deployed as TikTok ad campaigns, directing users straight to optimized collection pages.

"Launching a brand requires perfect coordination between web performance and marketing hooks. A fast Shopify storefront paired with authentic TikTok video reviews creates a high-trust shopping experience."

The Results

The full launch campaign generated outstanding metrics within the first 30 days:

  • Profitable launch campaign generating a 3.8x Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) on TikTok.
  • Calculated a +240% lift in typical baseline conversion rates compared to industry averages.
  • Full delivery of a high-performance, fully localized Shopify storefront prepared to scale.
