FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About eCommerce Marketing

Answers to common questions about paid social ads, Meta & TikTok campaigns, and ROAS optimization.

Which paid advertising channels does Storify manage?

We specialize in Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok Ads, Google Search & Shopping Ads, and dynamic catalog retargeting funnels.

How do you optimize ROAS and lower customer acquisition costs (CAC)?

We use dynamic creative testing (DCT), automated CBO campaign scaling, high-converting offer structures, audience segmentation, and retargeting sequences to maximize return on ad spend.

Do you handle ad creative design and copywriting?

Yes! Our in-house designers and copywriters write conversion-focused ad hooks, design visual static/carousel ads, and produce video ad concepts tailored for Meta and TikTok.

How is Meta Pixel & TikTok Conversion API (CAPI) set up for tracking?

We implement server-side tracking (CAPI) alongside standard pixel events to bypass iOS privacy restrictions and ensure 100% accurate attribution and purchase reporting.

What ad spend budget is recommended to start scaling?

We work with growing eCommerce brands starting from moderate testing budgets up to scale phases. We tailor campaign structures to maximize profitability at every budget tier.
